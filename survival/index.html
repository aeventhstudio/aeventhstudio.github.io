The game

Slide. Collect. Survive.

Slide

Every move carries you on until a wall stops you. Every room is a puzzle of stopping points.

Gather the Lumen

Fallen Lumen join you, each with an active and a passive power. Find their materials and evolve them.

Choose your loadout

You only have a few slots. Swap your Lumen when you rest at a light crystal, and choose where you can go and how long you last.

Explore

Interwoven Chaos Dungeons full of hidden paths, heart pieces and shortcuts. Knowledge is progress.

Survival

At the altars you face the evils themselves. Call the darkness, grab its essence and keep moving: in here your energy does not come back.

Never fight

Sins and evils roam the dungeons, and you cannot strike them. Learn their patterns, then slip past.

The story

The light of hope

The evils of the world have broken free. Their darkness drains everything of its energy until fields, creatures and machines freeze.

An old Lumen gave his life and became the Light Core. His spirit walks with you, the nameless Lumen.

The fallen Lumen, the evils' bright counterparts, wait in the Chaos Dungeons. Find them, face what is waiting, and bring the light back.

The look

Black, white, exact

Every room is drawn on a 16-pixel grid and scaled only in whole steps. Sharp on any screen, from a laptop to 4K.

  • 640 × 360 native
  • 32 × 18 cells per screen
  • 720p · 1080p · 1440p · 4K

The sound

Four channels, nothing more

Exploration NES style · 2 pulse, triangle, noise · 152 BPM · E minor · 63 s loop Composed by Alexander in FL Studio
