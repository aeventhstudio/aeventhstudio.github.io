Æventh:Survival

You are the last light in the darkness.

A Metroidvania without combat. As the nameless Lumen you slide through eight Chaos Dungeons, gather the fallen Lumen and survive the evils that have swallowed the world.

Move your light through the dark.

Gameplay

Slide. Collect. Survive.

Slide

Every move carries you on until a wall stops you. Every room is a puzzle of stopping points.

Gather the Lumen

Fallen Lumen join you, each with an active and a passive power. Find their materials and evolve them.

Choose your loadout

You only have a few slots. Swap your Lumen when you rest at a light crystal, and choose where you can go and how long you last.

Explore

Interwoven Chaos Dungeons full of hidden paths, heart pieces and shortcuts. Knowledge is progress.

Survival

At the altars you face the evils themselves. Call the darkness, grab its essence and keep moving: in here your energy does not come back.

Never fight

Sins and evils roam the dungeons, and you cannot strike them. Learn their patterns, then slip past.

Story

The last light

The evils of the world have broken free. Their darkness drains everything of its energy until fields, creatures and machines freeze.

An old Lumen gave his life and became the Light Core. His spirit walks with you, the nameless Lumen.

The Lumen who fell in battle wait in this world fallen into chaos, ready to join you and face the evils of the world one last time. Find them, face what lurks there, and bring the light back.

The creators

We are Alexander and Zarina.

What began as two strangers working on a game became a shared journey of love into the future. Despite the distance between Germany and Kazakhstan we never gave up, and now we are about to get married, just as our game is about to be released.

You could say that in our dark lives we found each other's light. That idea became our game. The light in Æventh stands for a hopeful future, and for not giving up despite every danger and obstacle.

Thank you for your interest in our game! <3
